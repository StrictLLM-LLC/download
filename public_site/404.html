Finish installing StrictLLM on your Mac

StrictLLM is not yet signed with Apple, so macOS will block the first launch. Follow these steps once to approve the app and start it.

info Only continue if you downloaded StrictLLM from this page. After approval, you can open it normally from Applications.
1

Try to open StrictLLM

Open StrictLLM Chat from your Applications folder. When macOS blocks it, click Done.

2

Open System Settings

Open the Apple menu in the top-left corner of your screen and choose System Settings.

3

Go to Privacy & Security

Select Privacy & Security in the System Settings sidebar.

4

Choose Open Anyway

Scroll to the Security section, find the StrictLLM message, and click Open Anyway.

5

Confirm the launch

In the confirmation dialog, click Open Anyway again.

6

Authenticate with macOS

Use Touch ID or click Use Password and enter an administrator password.

7

Allow Keychain access

When prompted, enter your Mac login password and choose Always Allow. StrictLLM will then finish starting. This is used to encrypt your API keys and credentials.
